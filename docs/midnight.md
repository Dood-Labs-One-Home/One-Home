# Midnight Build Club Direction

## Why This Project Belongs Here

One Home is being built as a connected ecosystem for identity, creativity, games, community, and personal memory.

The Midnight Build Club is a strong fit because the project is not only about adding features. It is about building experiences where people can understand what belongs to them, what is shared, and what should remain private.

One Home is designed to grow from real working prototypes into a more privacy-aware home for people, their stories, and the communities they choose to join.

## The One Home Vision

One Home is the front door.

It brings together:

* Dood Labs as the builder and ecosystem layer
* Dood ID as the identity direction
* Ember as the friendly guide
* Night Shots as the privacy-focused Memory Room
* Rollies Arcade and self-hosted games
* Future creator, community, project, and discovery spaces

The intended experience is simple:

```text
One Home
  -> Choose where you want to go
  -> Understand what you can do there
  -> Keep control of your identity and personal content
```

## Night Shots: The Privacy-Focused Project

Night Shots is the clearest privacy-focused part of the One Home ecosystem.

It is a memory and journaling experience built around personal photos, written stories, private journals, sharing choices, and the ability to revisit important moments without turning them into public social content.

The current prototype includes:

* Create Memory
* Photo upload
* Story and description fields
* Privacy choices
* Gallery view
* Journal view
* Search
* Favorites
* Sharing controls
* Password-protected shared links
* Recycle bin and restore behavior
* Themes and personal organization tools

The long-term direction is to strengthen ownership, privacy, and selective sharing without making the product difficult to use.

## Midnight Privacy Opportunities

Midnight is being explored as a future privacy layer where it offers a meaningful benefit.

The goal is not to force every feature into a privacy system immediately. The goal is to begin with the areas where personal control matters most.

Potential first milestones include:

1. Private memory access controls.
2. Private journal permissions.
3. Selective sharing with chosen people.
4. Privacy-aware identity-linked activity.
5. Proof of membership or access without exposing unnecessary personal information.
6. Clear ownership and visibility rules for personal content.

## What Makes This Different

One Home is not being designed as a collection of disconnected apps.

The project connects identity, memory, games, and community while keeping the experience understandable for people who are not technical.

The core idea is that people should be able to:

* Arrive somewhere welcoming
* Know what is public and private
* Choose how they participate
* Carry their identity across experiences
* Keep personal stories from becoming public by default
* Enjoy games and creative spaces without losing their sense of place

## Build Club Goals

During the Midnight Build Club process, the focus is to:

1. Present One Home as one understandable project repository.
2. Keep Night Shots positioned as the privacy-focused Memory Room.
3. Document the architecture, privacy model, and project roadmap.
4. Define practical privacy milestones instead of vague promises.
5. Preserve working prototypes while organizing them for future development.
6. Build toward meaningful user control over memories, journals, identity-linked activity, and sharing.

## Guiding Principles

* Privacy should be intentional and understandable.
* Personal memories should be private by default.
* Identity should support connection without requiring unnecessary exposure.
* Sharing should be chosen by the person who owns the content.
* Technology should make the experience clearer, not more confusing.
* Real working prototypes matter more than empty promises.
